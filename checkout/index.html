Yes. You control your media budget and pay Meta directly. We recommend starting with a sensible test budget and increasing it as performance warrants.

The £297/month covers the managed growth system. Ad spend is separate. SMS and AI usage charges can apply based on usage.

What does the AI do?

The AI responds to new enquiries, asks qualification questions, follows up and directs suitable prospects towards your booking calendar.

What's the process after I join?

After payment you continue to onboarding, provide the required business information and we begin building the campaign, website and automation system.
